# Group Vision Plan Summary

> **SYNTHETIC DATA — for portfolio/demo purposes only.** This document was generated for the
> Group Claims Pipeline portfolio project. It is not a real Mutual of Omaha or any other
> insurer's policy document, and describes no real coverage.

## Coverage Limits

The Group Vision Plan reimburses eligible vision claims up to a maximum of $500.00 per claim.
This limit covers eye exams, prescription eyeglasses (frames and lenses), and contact lenses in
combination, not as separate sub-limits, in this synthetic plan — a member who needs both an
exam and new eyeglasses in the same visit files one claim covering both, still capped at
$500.00 total rather than $500.00 per item. A claim requesting more than $500.00 is denied in
full against the plan's cap rather than partially reimbursed up to the limit, and the plan does
not average the exam cost separately from the eyewear cost to bring either component under the
cap individually. There is no separate annual aggregate cap beyond the per-claim limit, so a
member who needs a second, unrelated vision claim later in the same plan year (for example, a
lost-and-replaced pair of glasses) files it as an independent claim evaluated on its own merits.

## Exclusions

Laser vision correction surgery (LASIK and similar procedures) is excluded from this plan, as it
is treated as an elective procedure rather than corrective eyewear, even when a member's
optometrist recommends it as an alternative to glasses or contacts. Non-prescription
(cosmetic-only) contact lenses and sunglasses without a corrective prescription are excluded,
since the plan's purpose is to offset the cost of vision correction rather than eyewear in
general. Replacement eyewear purchased solely for style rather than due to a change in
prescription or physical damage is not covered, and a member cannot file a second claim for
replacement frames within the same plan year absent a qualifying prescription change or damage
event. Claims submitted more than 180 days after the date of service are denied for late
filing, measured from the date of purchase or exam, not the date the invoice was paid.

## Waiting Periods

A newly enrolled employee must wait 30 days from the enrollment's effective date before any
vision claim becomes eligible for benefits. Unlike the dental plan's tiered waiting periods,
this synthetic vision plan applies a single flat 30-day waiting period to all covered services,
since vision claims in this plan design do not distinguish between routine and complex care the
way dental claims do, and there is no equivalent of a "major service" category carrying an
extended waiting period here. A member who re-enrolls after a gap in coverage is treated as
newly enrolled for this purpose and does not carry over any waiting-period credit from a prior
enrollment period, consistent with how the other lines of coverage in this synthetic plan
family handle re-enrollment.

## Definitions

"Active coverage" means an enrollment record with status `ACTIVE` as of the date of service, not
the date the claim was submitted, so coverage that lapses after the date of service but before
the claim is filed does not retroactively disqualify an otherwise eligible claim. "Eligible
vision claim" means an eye exam performed by a licensed optometrist or ophthalmologist, or
prescription eyewear (glasses or contact lenses) issued pursuant to a current prescription no
more than 24 months old at the time of purchase, reflecting the plan's assumption that
prescriptions older than that are unlikely to still accurately reflect the member's vision.
